AI configuration

Your API key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent directly to Anthropic when generating minutes. It is never sent anywhere else.
Free, no signup with Anthropic needed — runs through Puter.js, which loads its own small script from js.puter.com the first time you use it. The first request may open a one-time Puter sign-in popup (free account, no card); after that it just works. Quality and speed vary by model and are outside this app's control.

Data

All meetings, transcripts, and settings are stored only on this device. Nothing is uploaded except the text sent to your chosen AI provider when you click Generate or Run AI check.
