How this app works

A quick walkthrough, start to finish. Every step below is something you can try right now.

1. Get your first minutes in 4 steps

  1. Tap New Meeting. Fill in whatever meeting details you have — all of them are optional, so skip what you don't know.
  2. Add the content: type Quick Notes, paste a transcript, use Record Meeting to speak it, Upload Recording for an existing audio/video file, or Attach material to pull text from a photo/PDF.
  3. Tap Generate minutes with AI. You'll see a short progress message, then land on the Review screen with agenda items, decisions, and an action-point register already filled in.
  4. Read through it, fix anything the AI got wrong (it's told never to invent decisions or deadlines, but always double-check), then tap Save meeting.

2. Three ways to turn notes into minutes

Generate minutes with AI — sends your notes to whichever AI provider you've picked in Settings. Best quality, especially for formal language and classifying decisions vs. discussion.

Auto-structure without AI (offline) — runs entirely on your device using keyword matching. Nothing is sent anywhere. Rougher: your own sentences are used as-is (not reworded), and it won't translate Hindi to English even if you've set English as the output language — only the AI path translates.

Skip AI — enter minutes manually — opens a blank Review screen for you to type everything in by hand. Also fully local.

3. Recording and attaching material

Record Meeting lets you record live with your microphone; if your browser supports it, you'll also see a live transcript as you speak (works best in Chrome; iPhone Safari records audio fine but usually won't transcribe live).

Upload Recording attaches an existing audio/video file and can transcribe it with AI, including speaker labels and timestamps where the audio allows.

Attach material (on New Meeting or Review) reads text out of photos, scans, and PDFs — handy for agenda papers or handwritten notes. Review the extracted text, fix any errors, then tap Insert into notes.

4. The Review screen

  • Every field is editable — agenda headings, discussion text, decisions, and the whole action-point table.
  • For Board Meetings, a quorum check appears automatically if you filled in total members and quorum required.
  • AI check flags missing owners, missing deadlines, unclear decisions, and possible duplicates — it never changes your text, only points things out.
  • Ask AI about this meeting answers questions using only that meeting's own content.
  • Listen to minutes reads the full minutes, a summary, decisions only, actions only, or pending matters only, out loud.
  • Minutes status (Draft → Circulated → Confirmed) matches how real minute books track approval at the next meeting.

5. Getting minutes out

Review & Export has Save, Word export, and Excel export (with separate sheets for details, attendance, agenda, decisions, action points, and a follow-up tracker).

Share & Print (its own tab) prints or saves as PDF via your device's print dialog, and shares by WhatsApp, Email, or your device's native share sheet — including sharing the actual Word file, not just text.

6. Finding things later

Meeting Library — every saved meeting, searchable by title, person, department, agenda, or keyword.

Action Tracker — every action point across all meetings in one place, with overdue/due/completed status you can update directly.

Insights — meetings per month, completion rates, and who's carrying the most open action points.

7. Settings worth knowing about

AI provider — Puter.js (free, no key, may show a one-time sign-in popup) or your own Anthropic API key. Whichever you pick applies to minute generation, AI check, Ask AI, and text extraction from photos/PDFs.

Default organisation and default meeting template save you re-typing them every time.

8. Privacy, in one paragraph

Everything you type, save, and export stays on this device (localStorage/IndexedDB) unless you explicitly tap one of the AI actions — Generate with AI, AI check, Ask AI, or Attach material's text extraction. Those send only the relevant text (or file) to your chosen provider. "Auto-structure without AI" and "Skip AI" never send anything anywhere.

AI configuration

Your API key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent directly to Anthropic when generating minutes. It is never sent anywhere else.
Free, no signup with Anthropic needed — runs through Puter.js, which loads its own small script from js.puter.com the first time you use it. The first request may open a one-time Puter sign-in popup (free account, no card); after that it just works. Quality and speed vary by model and are outside this app's control.

🔒 PIN protection (optional)

Encrypts your saved meetings on this device (AES-GCM) so they can't be read without your PIN. Off by default. If you forget your PIN, encrypted data cannot be recovered — there is no backdoor.

Data

All meetings, transcripts, and settings are stored only on this device. Nothing is uploaded except the specific content sent when you use an AI action (Generate, AI check, Ask AI, or extraction) — see the Privacy Dashboard for full details and to delete everything.
